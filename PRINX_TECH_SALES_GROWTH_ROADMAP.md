# Prinx Tech Sales Growth Roadmap

Goal: turn prinx.tz from a fast catalogue into a trusted local ecommerce store that creates enquiries, orders and repeat customers.

## Phase 1 — Brand & Trust Foundation
Status: implemented; deployment repair included

- Use **Prinx Tech** consistently across storefront, SEO and business settings.
- Replace fake review counts with genuine customer review data.
- Allow reviews only from customers with a completed order containing the product.
- Mark approved buyer reviews as **Verified Purchase**.
- Add review moderation in Admin.
- Add real aggregate ratings to product structured data.
- Show honest "No reviews" states until genuine reviews exist.

Success checks:
- Product page shows real rating/review count.
- Verified customer can submit a review.
- Admin can approve/reject it.
- Only approved reviews are public.
- Google Product schema contains aggregate rating only when real approved reviews exist.

## Phase 2 — WhatsApp Buying Assistance
Status: implemented and deployed

- "Ask about this product" WhatsApp CTA on every product page.
- Pre-filled product name, URL and price.
- Sticky mobile contact CTA without covering checkout controls.
- Track WhatsApp product enquiries as conversion events.

## Phase 3 — Stronger Homepage Merchandising
Status: implemented and deployed

- New Arrivals section.
- Best Sellers section based on real sales.
- Trending section based on recent product views/cart activity.
- Daily Deals driven by real sale pricing and end dates.
- Remove duplicated or low-value homepage content.

## Phase 4 — Stock & Delivery Urgency
Status: implemented; CI repair included with Phase 5

- Show exact low-stock messaging only from real inventory.
- "Only X left" when quantity is genuinely low.
- Clear delivery estimate by location.
- Delivery/pickup availability near Buy Now.

## Phase 5 — Trust & Policy Content
Status: implemented and deployed

- FAQ page.
- Warranty information page.
- Clear returns/refund page.
- Delivery information page.
- Make these pages visible near purchase controls and footer.

## Phase 6 — Payment Confidence
Status: implemented and deployed

- Show accepted payment methods beside Buy Now/checkout.
- Mobile money, card and bank/payment options from actual configured methods.
- Clear payment-security messaging.
- Remove any payment claim that is not actually available.

## Phase 7 — Local SEO & Buying Guides
Status: implemented; test assertion repair included with Phase 8

- Prinx Tech Dodoma landing page.
- Category landing pages targeting real local searches.
- Blog/buying-guide module.
- Internal links from guides to products/categories.
- Product SEO titles/descriptions use Prinx Tech consistently.

## Phase 8 — Product Comparison & Variants
Status: implemented; pending deployment verification

- Compare products.
- Proper color/storage/size variants where products have them.
- Variant-specific stock and images.
- Better related-product logic.

## Phase 9 — Conversion Measurement
- Track source: Google, Instagram, TikTok, WhatsApp, direct/referral.
- Track product views, add-to-cart, Buy Now, checkout and completed orders.
- Simple admin conversion report.
- Use real data to decide where marketing time and ad budget go.

## Implementation rule
Complete, test and deploy one phase before starting the next. Do not add fake scarcity, fake viewer counts or fabricated reviews.
